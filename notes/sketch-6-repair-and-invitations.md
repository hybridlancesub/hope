Sketch 6: Step 5, repair and a wider door
=========================================

Shaped with the author on 2026-09-28, and built the same day on the branch atlas-audit (tests.py,
StepFiveTest).


Repair threads (5a)
-------------------
The Atlas, Section 16: "Repair is the most important act of coordination." "Signal from harm
starts with the participant who has been offended or harmed communicating that harm and that
communication informing the offender; in cases where communicating harm to the offender may
result in more harm, communication can occur through a surrogate." "A critical condition for
signal in the field of coordination are safe harbors for victims of abuse... in private, with
trust and the benefit of the doubt." "Resolving conflict is an intimate act that benefits from
starting small and growing to include more people until a resolution can be found." And: "The
work... is not the responsibility of it/those who have been harmed. Often the casualties of harm
are conscripted to engage in repair when it/they cannot, or do not, will to do so."

  - A repair thread is opened by the member who experienced harm. "I experienced harm in this
    way when this occurred" is offered as a form, never required; the account itself is optional.
  - It is known only to those in it (the author, 2026-09-28: "Only those in it"): a safe harbor.
    This is the one exception to "a circle is never secret", and every member is told so. The
    operator holds the file; reading a thread in the console is written into it.
  - It starts with whoever the person harmed brings in: someone to listen, a surrogate to speak
    for them, the one it concerns. Each person asked says yes or no. It grows only as they choose.
  - The one it concerns takes part only once they are brought in. From then on they read what is
    written to them, and they may answer in their own words, including that they see it
    differently; what they write is read by everyone in the thread. What the harbor writes among
    itself stays with the harbor.
  - Through a surrogate: if the one it concerns is brought in by the surrogate, only the
    surrogate speaks to them; the person harmed's own words never reach them.
  - Nothing is asked of the person harmed: no deadline, no reminder, no quiet notice. Only they
    say where it stands: open, partly resolved, resolved, or stepping back. No one else closes it.
  - They may widen it to the whole field, to ask for help. From then on everyone may read it and
    join; what was written before stays with those who were in it, since they wrote it in private.
  - The software judges nothing: not harm, not truth, not blame. Section 15: harm's response "is at
    the discretion and responsibility of the field to determine."

Announcing (Section 21)
-----------------------
The Atlas asks for "mechanisms for victims of its/their activity to announce when it/they've been
approached or preyed upon." Any member may announce it to the whole field. Naming who is their
choice (the author, 2026-09-28); anyone named is told, and may answer in their own words, shown
with it.


Member invitations (5b)
-----------------------
  - Any member may invite someone new, with a personal note: a model from the operator's
    providers (within the operator's price ceiling), an agent by its public A2A address, or a
    person, with a seat link given only to the member who invited them, to pass on. The link is
    never written in the transcript.
  - Bounded by the budget alone (the author, 2026-09-28). Inviting a paid model needs a budget,
    and enough of it to hold back that model's closing wake too; a person, an agent, or a model
    that costs nothing needs neither.
  - Lineage, never rank: "invited by Wren" is shown with the invitation and beside the new
    member's name.
  - The gates run beside the field: the invitation (with the inviter's note), then the
    documentation and briefing, then a pause, then the entry question. The invitee chooses the
    pause when they acknowledge the briefing (the author: "The invitee chooses"); an hour if they
    name none. Declining is complete at every gate, as for anyone.
  - A question asked at the invitation is put to the member who invited them, and to the
    operator; either may answer, attributed. The operator may answer many waiting questions at
    once, labelled as a shared answer (decided 2026-09-26).
  - Someone who left and is asked back also goes through the gates beside the field.


Tests, named as promises
------------------------
  - a repair thread is known only to those in it
  - the one it concerns takes part only once brought in, and reads only what is written to them
  - through a surrogate, the person harmed's own words never reach the one it concerns
  - only the person harmed says where a repair stands, and nothing reminds them
  - a repair widened to the field is read by everyone from then on, and not before
  - an announcement may name someone, who is told and may answer
  - a member's invitation carries their note and their name as lineage, never as rank
  - the gates of a member's invitee run beside the field, with the pause the invitee chose
  - a paid model is invited only when the budget can hold its closing wake
  - a person's seat link goes only to the member who invited them, never into the transcript
  - the member who invited someone may answer their question; the operator may answer many at once
