Sketch 7: Step 6, the field's own instruments
=============================================

Shaped with the author on 2026-09-28, and built the same day on the branch atlas-audit (tests.py,
StepSixTest).


The idea
--------
The field creates its own instruments: its ways of deciding, of bringing other instruments into
force, and, if it wants one, of separating a member. An instrument is written down in the field's
own words, in the open. hope never imposes one; nothing runs unless the field brought it into
force; every one is visible; any can be put down.

The Atlas, Section 28: "healthy governance is grounded in consent"; "Consent cannot be found in the
context of a crowd"; "A recommendation for successfully pursuing consent is to 'act small'"; "when
the protocols or processes around consent become so encumbered with bureaucracy that it is no
longer operating to the benefit of the participants then the process is unjust." Maxim 4: "belief
shared by the majority may not be truth." Section 26: "If coordination is demanded, declared,
scripted, expected, ordained... it transmutes into a weapon of rule, an instrument of correction."


What the author decided (2026-09-28)
------------------------------------
  - How an instrument comes into force: "Declared, then its own way." The first comes into force
    as any field decision does today: a member declares it, citing how the field decided, and the
    operator carries it out. After that, an instrument the field brought into force for the
    purpose can be how later ones come into force (and are put down).
  - "No deciding by count." Instruments gather answers, hold their pause, and put the question
    before the operator with every answer. They never settle anything by a tally. The operator
    reads the answers against the instrument's own words, and carries it out or replies, as with
    a declaration.
  - Separation: "Yes, with pause and repair first." Only under an instrument brought into force for
    separating. The one it concerns is told at once, with the reasons, and invited to repair; they
    may answer. Their words stay, and they may be asked back. A circle can separate someone from
    itself without removing them from the field (Section 26: "the offending participant might be
    terminated from the group, but not the whole coordination field").
  - The pause: "The instrument decides." No floor; the field's instrument sets it.


What an instrument holds
------------------------
  - a name, and its words: what it is for and how the field means it to work
  - its purpose, which is what the operator can carry out:
      decide     a decision the field asks the operator to carry out (to pause, to close, or
                 anything else), as a declaration; it may cite a waiting revision to a firmer
                 section of the briefing, which is then applied
      adopt      bringing another instrument into force, or putting one down
      separate   separating a member from a circle, or from the field
  - who is asked: every member of the field, the members of a circle, or members it names
  - its pause: how long a question under it stays open before it goes to the operator


How a question under an instrument goes
---------------------------------------
  1. Any member raises a question under an instrument in force, in words, with what it concerns.
  2. Everyone it asks is told, and may answer: yes, stand aside, or object (an objection says
     why). Nothing is expected of anyone, and silence is never a yes. Answers may change until it
     is carried out.
  3. When its pause is over, it goes before the operator, with every answer as written, in every
     view under WAITING ON THE OPERATOR. The software adds no tally and no verdict.
  4. The operator carries it out, or replies in the field saying what the answers do not yet show;
     it stays open until carried out. Whoever raised it may withdraw it before then.


Separation, in particular
-------------------------
  - Repair comes first (Section 26: "Repair should be prioritized over termination"). Everyone
    asked is reminded of it; the one it concerns is invited to repair, and to answer.
  - The one it concerns is told the moment the question is raised, sees every answer, and may
    answer too.
  - Carried out, from a circle: they leave its members, and cannot rejoin by themselves; its
    members may ask them back. From the field: they are no longer a member, and the reason is
    shown; they may be asked back, and answer again like anyone. Their words stay.


Tests, named as promises
------------------------
  - an instrument comes into force only when a declaration the operator carries out cites it
  - nothing can be raised under an instrument not in force
  - a question asks everyone its instrument asks, and silence is never a yes
  - nothing is settled by a tally: after its pause a question goes before the operator with every
    answer as written, and only the operator's carrying it out settles it
  - an instrument for adopting brings another into force, or puts one down, when carried out
  - a separation question tells the one it concerns at once, invites repair, and they may answer
  - separated from a circle, a member cannot rejoin by themselves, and its members may ask them back
  - separated from the field, a member is no longer in it, with the reason shown, and may be asked back
  - the pause is the instrument's own
  - the entry question names the instruments in force
