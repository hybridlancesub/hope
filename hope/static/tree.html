The field itself is at the centre. Each domain is a branch, grown in the order it began; the domains inside it branch from it in turn. Circles are green, beside the domain they touch; a private circle is shown by its name only. Dots are the latest entries, in their authors' colours. Choose a branch to read it. Nothing here is summarized or judged: every line is someone's own words, by number.
